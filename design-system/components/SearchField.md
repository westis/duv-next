# SearchField

An underlined search input with a visually hidden label.

## When to use
Runner and race search: in the header (`onHeader`) and at the top of search pages.

## What you provide
`placeholder`, an accessible `label` (defaults to "Search runner or race"), `onChange`, `onHeader` on the header band.

## Rules
Underline only: `rule-hair` in `control-border` (`header-muted` on the header). Square, no fill, no icon-only search.
