# StatStrip

Key facts or the podium as big condensed figures under a 6px ink rule, with hairline dividers.

## When to use
Under a `PageHeader`: the podium on a race page, best mark / races / years on a runner page, finishers / countries on an edition. Two to four items.

## What you provide
`items` of `{label, value, unit?, detail?}` — `value` is the figure only ("187.207"), `unit` follows small ("km"), `detail` names who.

## Rules
Figures use tabular digits. Stack to one column on phones.
