# RecordMarker

A compact, square marker placed right after a performance: WR, AR, NR, CR or PB.

## When to use
In results, runner pages and rankings whenever a performance is a record or personal best.

## What you provide
`kind`, optional `category` ("NR M50"), optional `body` ("IAU AR", outlined) and a `title` explaining the source.

## Rules
WR/AR/NR are solid `brand`; CR solid `cr` with `cr-fg`; PB outlined in `pb`. 18px tall, never larger than the figure it follows.
