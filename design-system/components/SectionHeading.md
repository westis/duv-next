# SectionHeading

An uppercase `section-title` on a 3px ink rule, with an optional muted aside on the right.

## When to use
To open each section of a page: Results, All editions, Records, Calendar.

## What you provide
`children` (one or two words), `aside` (a count or short note), `id` for in-page links, `as` (`h2` default or `h3`).
