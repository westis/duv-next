# PageHeader

The header of race, runner, country and calendar pages: a red kicker and an uppercase `page-title` over 8 columns, meta right-aligned over 4.

## When to use
Once per page, right under the site header. Follow it with a `StatStrip` (key facts) or a `SectionHeading`.

## What you provide
- `title` — the race or runner name, without the edition number.
- `kicker` — distance or type · surface · edition, short.
- `meta` — up to three lines: date, place, finishers.

## Rules
Kicker in `brand-ink`, title in `fg`. On phones meta drops under the title, left-aligned, and the title shrinks to 40px.
