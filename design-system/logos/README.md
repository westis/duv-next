# Logos

The DUV logo: a runner drawn in three strokes (red, yellow and the head) beside the lowercase "duv" script. Both are 4096 × 1920 PNG with a transparent background, from the duv-next repository.

- `duv_logo_with_name.png` — navy-black lettering. Use on `bg` and `surface` in the light theme.
- `duv_logo_with_name_white.png` — white lettering. Use on the `header` band in both themes and on `bg` / `surface` in the dark theme.

Give the logo clear space of at least its own lettering height on every side. In the header it is 28px tall; on a board or cover 56px. The red and yellow strokes are fixed colours of the mark: do not recolour, outline or redraw it.
